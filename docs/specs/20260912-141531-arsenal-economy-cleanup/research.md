# Research: Arsenal and Economy Cleanup

## Decision: delete Bomb Net fully

**Rationale**: It is currently catalogue, inventory, price, child pattern, shop/UI, AI, test, and roadmap state. Removing it leaves Cluster Bomb focused.

**Alternatives considered**: Zero ammunition or hidden UI leaves dead paths.

## Decision: terrain-only Bunker Buster

**Rationale**: Use zero health damage with much larger bounded deep/directional existing terrain deformation and settlement.

**Alternatives considered**: Giant spherical crater loses identity; second terrain system expands scope.

## Decision: actual damage plus deterministic placement

**Rationale**: Credit health delta after saturation. Configured-player resolution order resolves simultaneous eliminations; draws get no winner award.

**Alternatives considered**: Nominal damage rewards overkill; kill/winner bonuses obscure the equation.
