# Research Decisions

**Deployment**: a surviving descending carrier deploys once; Cluster uses dense 3×3-like offsets and Net a broad 4×4 grid. This extends MIRV ownership while preserving distinct density and footprint without fuse controls.

**Roller**: retain landing momentum, sample local terrain heights for downhill direction, and end on a bounded stop/time/distance rule. **Bunker Buster**: convert contact into short bounded impact-velocity displacement then one narrow/deep normal explosion. Both remain deterministic without rigid-body or underground simulation.

**Presentation**: extend existing aggregate/coalesced MIRV treatment with read-only Roller/penetration state only as needed.
